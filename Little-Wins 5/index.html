At Little Wins, behavioural science helps guide how we understand learning, behaviour, emotional regulation, and development within everyday environments. Common Questions

Questions families often ask

We want you to feel confident before you begin. Here are answers to the questions we hear most.

My child is hesitant about therapy — what makes your approach different?+
At Little Wins, we prioritize building trust and connection before expecting change. We meet children where they are, creating a space that feels safe, supportive, judgement-free (and fun!). By blending science with play, we help children feel understood, empowered, and motivated — at their own pace.
What happens in a typical therapy session?+
Sessions are structured yet flexible, blending play, creativity, and evidence-based activities tailored to your child's goals. Some sessions focus on 1:1 skill-building, while others may involve practicing skills in the community. Every session is designed around your child's comfort level.
How do I know what approach will work for my child?+
Every child is unique. There's no one-size-fits-all approach. Our therapists begin with a thorough assessment to understand your child's strengths, needs and family context. From there we design an individualized plan using evidence-based strategies through our integrated care model, while collaborating closely with families and relevant professionals to ensure support is consistent, holistic, and tailored to your child's needs.
What role does motivation play in my child's learning?+
Every child is guided by their own interests and motivations. Our child-centred approach means we take time to understand what truly motivates each child and family. By weaving these motivations into therapy, we create a space where children feel seen, engaged, and willing to try.
What is my role as a parent or caregiver?+
While we guide the therapeutic approach, families are always active collaborators. Through ongoing guidance, training, and collaboration, we support families in extending learning beyond sessions — so progress continues at home, in school, and in everyday life.
Do you offer online sessions?+
Yes! We are available in-person in Mumbai and online globally. Our online sessions are just as engaging and effective, ensuring families worldwide can access quality behavioural health support from the comfort of their home.
